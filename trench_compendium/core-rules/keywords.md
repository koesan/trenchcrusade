# Keywords

Rules governing all Keywords in the game are listed here. A model, a piece of Battlekit, a Marker or a terrain piece can have 1 or more Keywords. They define the core abilities, rules, troop types and damage categories in the game. Keywords appear in the rules in CAPITALS to make it easy for you to spot them.

Keywords have two benefits: they act as Tags and may confer an Effect. A Keyword that confers an Effect also acts as a Tag.

**Tags:** A Keyword that can be referred to by other rules.

**Effect:** A Keyword that confers a special rule to a model that has the Keyword, or has a piece of Battlekit that has that Keyword.
