# Preparing To Play

On this page

[](https://trench-companion.com/compendium/scenarios/rules/preparing-to-play#)[Scenario List](https://trench-companion.com/compendium/scenarios/rules/preparing-to-play#Scenario%20List)

To play a game of Trench Crusade, you will need to recruit a Warband, find an opponent, and then decide which scenario you and your opponent want to play. If you’re playing a campaign game, the scenario you will play will be determined using the rules.

If you’re playing a one-off game, you can choose which scenario you want to play; if you can’t agree, roll-off and the winner can pick the scenario you will use. Once you’ve chosen your scenario, the rules in this section will explain how to set up and play the game.

When you are ready to play the scenario, you will need to bring along the models for the force you will use in the game, some terrain pieces, rules, dice and markers, and a copy of the rulebook.
