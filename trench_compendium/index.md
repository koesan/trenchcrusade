## About Trench Crusade

During the First Crusade the armies of the Church captured the Holy City. Beneath the most sacred of temples, the Knights Templar discovered hidden vaults. Within their shadowed depths lay an ancient demonic artefact. Overcome by greed and the weakness in their hearts, the Templars fell to their knees. They had found a new lord.

Witnessing the unholy wisdom revealed by the artefact, they turned to darkness, trafficking with devils and committing all manner of unspeakable rites. This was the Ultimate Heresy. Jerusalem was reduced to a depraved pit where the Infernal seeped into our Earthly realm, Hell and our mortal plane are now entwined in corruption.

For eight centuries the Church has waged its crusade to take back the Holy City. The landscape has been utterly devastated, scarred by thousands of miles of trenches, mud, and craters. The war is now waged by armies wielding terrifying weaponry. Both sides command supernatural beings of such immense power that they are nighunstoppable. Even with such incalculable might the armies are at a stalemate.
