# Warbands

In this section, we will take a detailed look at six of the Warbands that can be used in games of Trench Crusade, and provide all of the information you need to recruit one. Once you have collected your Warband, you can use it to fight battles using the rules from the Trench Crusade Rulebook or this Compendium.
